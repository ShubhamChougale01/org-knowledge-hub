# 🏢 Tech Service Company — Organizational Hierarchy

> **6 Departments · 7 Hierarchy Levels**  
> Departments: HR · Marketing · Technical · Sales · Delivery · Finance

---

## 📊 Hierarchy Overview

| Level | Label | Scope |
|-------|-------|-------|
| **Level 1** | C-Suite | Company-wide leadership |
| **Level 2** | C-Level / VP | Department heads |
| **Level 3** | Directors | Department strategy & operations |
| **Level 4** | Managers | Team management |
| **Level 5** | Senior ICs | Senior individual contributors |
| **Level 6** | Mid ICs | Mid-level individual contributors |
| **Level 7** | Entry Level | Juniors, trainees & interns |

---

## 🔝 Level 1 — C-Suite

| Role | Reports To |
|------|-----------|
| **CEO** *(Chief Executive Officer)* | Board of Directors |

---

## 🏛️ Level 2 — C-Level / VP *(Department Heads)*

All roles report directly to the **CEO**.

| Department | Role |
|------------|------|
| 🧑‍💼 HR | **CHRO** — Chief Human Resources Officer |
| 📣 Marketing | **CMO** — Chief Marketing Officer |
| 💻 Technical | **CTO** — Chief Technology Officer |
| 💰 Sales | **CSO** — Chief Sales Officer |
| 🚚 Delivery | **CDO / VP** — Delivery Head |
| 📊 Finance | **CFO** — Chief Financial Officer |

---

## 🔗 Finance — Sub-Teams

Finance operates across multiple sub-functions under the CFO:

| Sub-Team | Responsibility |
|----------|----------------|
| **Accounting** | Books, ledgers, reconciliation |
| **FP&A** *(Financial Planning & Analysis)* | Budgeting, forecasting, P&L |
| **Payroll** | Salary processing *(shared with HR)* |
| **Billing & Invoicing** | Client invoices, collections |
| **Taxation & Compliance** | GST, TDS, audits |
| **Treasury** | Cash flow, investments |

---

## 🔄 Cross-Department Relationships with Finance

```
Sales      ──→  Finance   (deals, contracts, revenue booking)
Delivery   ──→  Finance   (project billing, cost tracking)
HR         ──→  Finance   (payroll, headcount budgets)
Technical  ──→  Finance   (capex, infra costs, licenses)
Marketing  ──→  Finance   (campaign budgets, ROI reporting)
```
